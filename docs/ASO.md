## Phrases that carry the pitch

works offline · no upload · no internet needed · private · files stay on your phone · no watermark on results

## Words to avoid

- Anything that promises layout-perfect PDF to Word. The tool rebuilds text, and the listing says so.
- "Unlimited" or "free forever" — there is a free allowance and a premium plan.
- Format names the app does not write, such as RAR (it only reads), FLAC or OGG output (it reads them, and writes MP3, M4A, AAC and WAV).
